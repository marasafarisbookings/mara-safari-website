Who We Are

Based in Nairobi, Kenya, Masai Mara Safari Bookings is a premier licensed tour operator dedicated to crafting unforgettable luxury adventures across East Africa's most iconic game reserve. We merge absolute comfort with raw wilderness tracking.

Our Premium Custom Fleet

We own and maintain a fleet of custom-built 4x4 open-roof safari land cruisers. Every vehicle is specifically engineered for rough savanna terrain, outfitted with heavy-duty off-road suspension, custom camera beanbag stability mounts, internal device charging sockets, and a built-in cooler box to keep your bush drinks perfectly chilled.

KPSGA Certified Tour Guides

Our expert driver-guides are lifelong wilderness trackers native to the region. Every team member holds active professional Bronze or Silver certifications from the Kenya Professional Safari Guides Association (KPSGA). They possess deep knowledge of animal behavior patterns, migration timelines, bird taxonomy, and local cultural heritage tracking protocols.

Sustainable Eco-Tourism

We partner strictly with eco-certified luxury tented camps and boutique bush lodges that give back directly to the local community. Our game drives strictly follow wildlife protection regulations, ensuring that our presence preserves the natural habitats of the Big Five for generations to come.
